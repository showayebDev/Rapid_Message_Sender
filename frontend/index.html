📖 User & Safety Guide

⚡ Quick Start Instructions

  1. Select or type your target text message in the Message Content area.
  2. Configure Repeat Count, Interval (ms), and Start Delay.
  3. Click 🚀 Start Sending and immediately switch focus to your desired target application (e.g. Discord, Telegram, WhatsApp Web, Slack, Notepad).
  4. Wait for the initial countdown delay (default 2s) to finish.

🛡️ Emergency Fail-Safe Controls

  • Global Hotkey Ctrl+Q: Press Ctrl + Q anywhere on your operating system to trigger instant emergency abort.
  • Mouse Top-Left Corner Fail-Safe: Slam your mouse cursor into the extreme top-left corner pixel of your main monitor (0,0) to immediately halt dispatch.
  • Safety Floor: The execution speed interval is hard-locked to a minimum of 200 ms to protect against rate-limits.
